# Clear Writing: customer discovery kit

Use this before building more launch features. Do not pitch until the person has described their current workflow. Ask permission before recording; do not collect confidential customer messages.

## Target for the first round

Small teams that regularly write to customers, applicants, partners, or colleagues. Interview 3–5 people who personally do this work or choose the tools for their team.

## 20-minute interview

1. What kinds of messages or notes did you write in the last work week?
2. Which one took the most effort? What made it difficult?
3. Tell me about the last time that happened, step by step.
4. What did you do to get it finished? Which tools or people were involved?
5. How often does this happen, and roughly how much team time does it take?
6. What happens if the message is unclear, late, or has the wrong tone?
7. What have you tried already? What did you pay for, if anything?
8. What data or customer information would you never put into an AI writing tool?
9. Who would approve a tool like this, and what security or legal review would they need?
10. If this problem were solved, how would you know it saved time or improved the result?
11. Would you try a short pilot using non-sensitive sample text? What would block that?
12. What would a useful paid pilot need to include for you to consider it?

Avoid asking “Would you use this?” or “Do you like this idea?” Record exact examples, existing spend, frequency, and concrete next steps instead.

## Decision after 3–5 interviews

- **Continue this segment:** same costly job appears repeatedly and at least one qualified buyer agrees to a dated pilot or concrete purchase step.
- **Narrow or change segment:** pain is real but workflow, buyer, privacy needs, or budget differs materially.
- **Pause this offer:** most evidence is hypothetical, the task is infrequent/low-cost, or users will not share even safe sample text.

Do not contact interviewees on the owner's behalf from this document. Save no call recordings or customer content without explicit consent and a retention plan.
