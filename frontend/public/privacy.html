Privacy notice · Updated 16 November 2026

Your drafts stay yours.

This notice explains what Clear Writing handles when you use the writing demo or, when enabled, a signed-in workspace.

Text you choose to process

Your draft stays in this browser tab. Clear Writing sends it to its backend only after you choose Rewrite or Summarise and confirm both on-screen notices. The backend forwards the request to Google Gemini to produce the result. The app does not save submitted drafts or results in its database.

Do not submit passwords, health details, financial details, confidential business information, or other sensitive personal data. Google’s handling can vary by API plan; read the Gemini API terms before sending work content.

Browser storage

The draft, previous draft, workspace selection, sign-in tokens, and unsaved voice guidance or phrases may be kept in this tab’s session storage so the app can work across reloads. They are cleared when you sign out for account data or close the browser tab. Use Clear draft to remove the saved draft sooner.

Workspace information

When accounts are enabled, the service stores your email address, a password hash, workspace name, shared voice guidance, protected terms and phrases, team memberships, invitation status, and monthly AI request counts. Invitation tokens are stored as hashes. Emails for verification, password recovery, and invitations are sent through the mail service configured by the operator.

Optional getting-started email is off by default. If you opt in, Clear Writing sends one welcome email after verification or after you enable the preference later; it does not send recurring marketing emails. Your choice and consent timestamps are stored with your account, and you can turn it off from Email preferences in your signed-in workspace.

Protected terms help preserve the exact spelling and capitalization of product names you list. When a term appears in a draft, the app checks the generated result and rejects it if the term changed. The term list is sent to Google Gemini only after you confirm processing, together with the draft.

Separate free-tool signup forms may be enabled for occasional product updates. They are optional and do not block tool access. If enabled, the form sends your email, consent, product name, and page path to LeadHub for double opt-in; it does not send the draft text. Unsubscribing suppresses marketing email across EatItAll products. The forms stay hidden until the operator configures the HTTPS LeadHub URL and approves this notice.

Monthly request counts are shared by the workspace. If an account is deleted, its memberships and invitations are removed. An owned workspace is deleted only if no other members remain; otherwise ownership must be transferred or the other members removed first. Shared settings and usage remain with a workspace that continues for its team.

Service providers and retention

The website and API are configured to run on Render. Submitted writing is processed by Google Gemini. Account email is handled by the mail provider configured for the service. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices.

Workspace information remains while the workspace is active. Owners can delete an eligible account from workspace settings. Technical service providers may process connection and operational data needed to run and protect the service.

Who should use the service

Clear Writing is intended for adults aged 18 or older and professional writing. Review AI output before relying on it; it can be inaccurate or change nuance.
