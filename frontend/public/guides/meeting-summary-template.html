Meeting notes · Reusable template

Send a meeting recap with clear owners.

A useful recap is a record of what people decided and agreed to do. Keep decisions, tasks, open questions, and tentative ideas separate so readers do not mistake a discussion for a commitment.

Use this structure

Subject: [Project] — meeting recap — [date]

Hi everyone,

Thanks for meeting. Here is the short recap.

Decisions
• [Decision, including any condition]

Actions
• [Task] — [Owner] — due [date, time zone]
• [Task] — [Owner] — due [date, time zone]

Open questions
• [Question] — [person to confirm] — check by [date]

Next check-in: [date and purpose]

Please reply with corrections by [reasonable date].

Best,
[Name]

Three checks before sharing

  1. Confirm each decision with the people who made it; label unresolved items as open.
  2. Give every action one owner and a realistic date. If no owner was agreed, mark it unassigned instead of guessing.
  3. Limit distribution to people who need the notes. Remove private or sensitive discussion that is not needed for the task.

For a fast recap, begin with decisions and actions. Add background only when a reader needs it to understand why a task exists.
