# Draftloom market research

Checked 2026-11-16. Public pricing changes by region/offer; verify before publishing prices. Review themes below summarize public G2 reviewer comments, which are self-selected and not a representative survey.

## Target and pain hypothesis

Target: small client-facing marketing/operations teams that repeatedly edit customer emails, support replies, and internal drafts to match approved terminology and tone. Pain hypothesis: repeated rewrites consume time and create inconsistent or over-edited copy. Validate with five buyer interviews and a workflow observation before claiming measured savings.

**Positioning:** Draftloom helps small teams turn an existing draft into a clearer, on-brand message while keeping the original visible and requiring a human to accept every change.

## Implications and unknowns

- Compete on controlled edits, source/edited comparison, terminology protection, and team-approved style—not generic text generation.
- A differentiator is only real if the current code and blind user trials prove it. Do not claim Draftloom is more accurate or faster yet.
- Verify current deployment, AI provider cost/data handling, pricing, terms, and market willingness to pay. Interview evidence is not in this repo yet.
- Competitor weaknesses are review observations, not claims that every user has the problem. G2 pages disclose reviews are collected/hosted on G2 and some may be incentivized.
