# Clear Writing — one-page product brief

## Product

Clear Writing helps people make a draft easier to understand while keeping the author in control. Users can rewrite or summarize text, select a tone and professional format, add bounded reader/goal context, review linked sentence changes, and export Markdown, text or Word documents.

## Target users

Professionals and small teams writing customer emails, internal updates, meeting recaps and other short business text. The exact buyer and willingness to pay still need interviews.

## Current offer hypothesis

An individual or small-workspace subscription with usage limits, plus a team plan if seat-based collaboration proves useful. Test price and provider costs with real users before enabling paid access. No live subscription or paid revenue is claimed.

## Differentiation to test

Reviewable edits with exact sentence links and explanations, practical business formats, private local browser tools, and explicit control over when a draft is sent to the AI provider. These are product choices, not a verified superiority claim.

## Safety and readiness

Drafts are sent to Google Gemini only after the user chooses the AI action. Email sending is not performed. Accounts and test-mode billing exist in local code but remain disabled on the live demo. Production release still needs verified email delivery, database backups/restore, monitoring, approved legal/contact details, buyer validation, and service configuration.

## Success test

Interview target users, observe whether they complete a work draft and use the result, measure repeat use and provider cost, then validate a paid pilot. Continue only if users trust the review flow and the delivered value covers support and infrastructure cost.
